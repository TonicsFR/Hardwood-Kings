How to Play

Solo controls

  • WASD or Arrows: move
  • Shift: sprint (uses stamina)
  • J / Space: hold to shoot and release on the green. On defense this jumps to block or rebound.
  • K: dribble move on offense, steal on defense
  • Esc / P: pause
  • Gamepad: left stick moves, A shoots, X does moves or steals, RT/RB sprints

The shot meter

  • Hold shoot to rise, then let go when the bar hits the bright green band.
  • Releasing inside the band is a green, which goes in unless it's blocked.
  • The band shrinks when a defender is close and in front of you, so you can see how contested you are.
  • Your 3PT and Mid-Range ratings set the band's size. A stepback briefly opens it up.
  • Press shoot near the rim to lay it in. Sprint in with a good Dunk rating to throw it down.

Dribble moves

  • Crossover: press K while moving sideways.
  • Spin: press K while sprinting. It slips past the defender's body.
  • Stepback: press K while moving away from the hoop, then shoot.
  • Chain moves together for a better chance of breaking ankles. A stumbling defender can't contest.
  • Moves cost stamina. A tired player is slower and has a smaller green window.

Rules

  • Shots are worth 2s and 3s. Games run to 11, 15 or 21 (ranked is 21).
  • After a score the other player checks the ball at the top.
  • After a steal or defensive rebound you must take it back behind the arc before you can shoot.
  • The shot clock is 14 seconds and resets to 10 when a shot hits the rim.
  • On defense, stay between your man and the hoop. Reaching for a steal and missing leaves you slow for a moment.
